# LearnPeak | AI Tools Built for Your Curriculum


**LearnPeak** is an AI-powered learning system built around your curriculum, with textbook chat, quizzes, AR, and science-backed study strategies like spaced repetition for long-term memory.

LearnPeak goes beyond generic AI assistance by focusing on the student's actual curriculum and educational content. It provides a collection of learning tools designed to help students interact with their learning material in more effective ways.

### What LearnPeak Offers

- 📚 **Ask Your Book** — Ask questions about curriculum books and learning material using AI.
- 🧠 **AI Quiz Generation** — Generate quizzes to practice and test understanding
- 🥽 **Learn with AR** — Explore educational concepts through AR and 3D models.
- 🔁 **Science-Backed Learning** — Apply evidence-based study strategies such as spaced repetition to improve learning and retention.

## What Is LearnPeak?

LearnPeak is an AI-powered learning platform designed to make studying more interactive, personalized, and effective by putting the student's **curriculum at the center of the learning experience**.

Instead of treating education as a collection of generic questions and answers, LearnPeak is built around the material students are actually expected to learn at school. Its AI tools can work with curriculum-specific books and educational content, allowing students to ask questions, practice their knowledge, and explore concepts in ways connected to what they are studying.

### Built Around Your Curriculum

**Curriculum textbooks are built into the platform**, allowing students to directly use the platform without even having to manually provide all of their learning material.

LearnPeak is also designed to support additional learning sources. In the future, students will be able to add their own materials, such as **class notes, study guides, worksheets, teacher-provided materials, and additional textbooks**, and use them with LearnPeak's AI-powered learning tools.

The goal of LearnPeak is not simply to give students answers. It is to help them **understand what they learn, actively practice it, and retain it for longer** by combining AI-powered learning tools with evidence-based study strategies.

At its core, LearnPeak brings together three elements:

- **📚 Your Curriculum** — The school books and educational content you are actually studying.
- **🤖 Curriculum-Focused AI** — AI tools designed to work with and understand that learning material.
- **🧠 Effective Learning Strategies** — Evidence-based approaches that help students learn and retain knowledge more effectively.

This makes LearnPeak a **curriculum-focused learning system**, rather than simply a general-purpose AI chatbot with educational use cases.


## The Problem LearnPeak Solves

### 1. Generic AI Doesn't Know Your Curriculum

General-purpose AI can explain almost anything, but it does not automatically know **exactly what a student is expected to learn** from their curriculum or textbook. An answer can be correct in general while using different terminology, including information outside the curriculum, or going beyond what is expected in exams.

### 2. Students Need Help When They Get Stuck

Students regularly encounter **difficult concepts and questions** that require a clearer or more personalized explanation than their learning material provides. They may also have questions sparked by curiosity that could help them understand a topic more deeply. However, **teachers are not always available** whenever a student needs clarification.

### 3. Learning Tools Are Often Separated

Students may need different tools to ask questions, practice what they have learned, explore concepts visually, and apply effective study strategies. When these tools are spread across different platforms, students have to switch between services and repeatedly provide their learning context.

LearnPeak brings these needs together around the student's **actual curriculum and learning material**, providing AI-powered tools and learning strategies in one place.

### 4. Most Students Don't Know How to Study Effectively

Most students rely on passive study methods without knowing about evidence-based strategies that can improve learning and retention. Even students who have heard of techniques such as **active recall** and **spaced repetition** may not know how to apply them effectively or consistently.

## Features

LearnPeak brings together several AI-powered and evidence-based learning tools designed to help students understand, practice, explore, and retain their curriculum.

### 📚 Ask Your Book

**Ask Your Book** lets students interact with their curriculum textbooks through AI. Instead of searching through a textbook manually or asking a general-purpose AI without curriculum context, students can ask questions directly about the material they are studying.

Students can:

* Ask questions about lessons and concepts from their curriculum books.
* Get explanations based on the relevant learning material.
* Ask follow-up questions to explore a concept further.
* Clarify difficult topics or questions that arise while studying.

Supported curriculum textbooks are already **built into LearnPeak**, so students can start using Ask Your Book without manually uploading their books.

### 🧠 Quiz Generation

LearnPeak can generate **AI-powered quizzes based on curriculum learning material**, allowing students to actively practice what they have learned and test their understanding.

Instead of simply rereading a lesson, students can use generated quizzes to recall information, identify areas they need to review, and prepare for assessments.

### 🥽 Learn with AR

**Learn with AR** allows students to explore educational concepts through **Augmented Reality and 3D models**.

Visualizing a concept in 3D can provide a different way to understand subjects that may be difficult to grasp through written explanations or traditional textbook illustrations alone.

### 🔁 Science-Backed Learning

LearnPeak incorporates **evidence-based learning strategies** into the learning experience to help students learn and retain information more effectively.

These include strategies such as:

* **Spaced Repetition** — reviewing information at increasing intervals to support long-term retention.
* **Active Recall** — actively retrieving information from memory instead of simply rereading it.

LearnPeak aims to make these strategies more practical and accessible to students rather than leaving them as concepts they have to discover and figure out how to apply on their own.

### 👤 Personalized Learning Experience

Students can create an account and provide information about themselves and their learning context, allowing LearnPeak to provide a more **personalized learning experience**.

Their learning activity can also be saved, including things such as chat history and generated quizzes, so they can return to previous learning sessions when needed.


## Example Use Cases

LearnPeak can support students in different situations throughout their learning journey. Here are some examples of how its tools can be used in practice.

### 1. Understanding a Difficult Part of a Lesson

**Situation:** You are studying a lesson and come across a concept that is difficult to understand.

**With LearnPeak:** You use **Ask Your Book** to ask the AI about the difficult part and have it explained in simpler terms. You can then ask follow-up questions to clarify details or explore the concept further.

**Result:** You gain a clearer understanding of the difficult concept and can continue studying with confidence.

### 2. Exploring a Complex Diagram or Concept

**Situation:** You encounter a complex diagram or concept in your science lesson that is difficult to visualize from a textbook.

**With LearnPeak:** You use **Learn with AR** to search for relevant 3D models, choose the model that best fits what you are studying, and explore it interactively.

**Result:** You can visualize and explore the concept in three dimensions, giving you a better understanding of what you are studying.

### 3. Practicing Before an Exam

**Situation:** You have an upcoming exam and want to practice, especially on the parts of the curriculum you find difficult.

**With LearnPeak:** You use **Quiz Generation** to generate quizzes based on the relevant material. After solving them, you can review your mistakes and use the AI-generated overview of incorrect answers to understand the areas that need more attention.

**Result:** You identify your weak areas, understand your mistakes, and become better prepared for the exam.

### 4. Learning How to Study More Effectively

**Situation:** You want to make your study time more effective instead of simply spending more time studying.

**With LearnPeak:** You explore the **Study Strategies** feature to learn the basics of effective studying and evidence-based strategies such as active recall and spaced repetition.

**Result:** You gain practical knowledge about how to study more effectively, helping you make better use of your study time and retain what you learn for longer.
